# JezSy System Design & Brand Theme Specification

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic Overview
- **Brand Name**: JezSy / JezSy by Ms. Jholy
- **Core Aesthetic**: High-End Boutique Luxury, Coquette Chic, Modern Glam, & Elegant Botanical.
- **Design Impression**: Radiant, sophisticated, romantic, and warm-toned with rich metallic accents, refined serif accents, and dark high-contrast elements.

## Color System Tokens

### Primary Palette (Warm Metallic & Luxury Neutrals)
- **Primary Gold / Accent (`--accent`)**: `#D4AF37` / `#AA8C2C` (Warm Champagne Gold)
- **Rose Gold / Blush Pink**: `#F4C2C2` / `#E8A0B8` (Soft Romantic Blush)
- **Obsidian / Charcoal (`--charcoal`)**: `#1F1C18` / `#121212` (Deep Charcoal Black)
- **Cream / Warm Alabaster (`--cream`)**: `#FDFBF7` / `#F5EEDC` (Soft Luxury Warm Cream)
- **Soft Warm Gray / Muted Border (`--border-color`)**: `#EFE9DB` / `#DCD3B6`

### Typography & Lettering
- **Headings & Display**: Elegant Serif / High-contrast display script (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or Outfit).
- **Body & Controls**: Clean, legible modern sans-serif (Inter, System UI) with comfortable line height and crisp weight hierarchy.

## Visual Design Elements
1. **Embossed & Metallic Accents**: Subtly shaded gold borders (`--border-color`), pill badges with soft metallic glow, and polished high-value CTA buttons.
2. **Coquette & Botanical Motifs**: Ribbon bow accents, subtle heart embellishments, olive leaf/laurel wreaths for achievement/rewards, and soft 4-point sparkle star indicators (`✨`).
3. **Pill Badges & Cards**: Rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 20px`), glassmorphic translucency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), subtle drop shadows, and rich HSL status colors.
