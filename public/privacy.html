Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

JezSy Boutique ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application and related boutique services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, and authentication records.
  • Reservation & Transaction Data: Rental dates, selected garments, payment status, deposit amounts, and transaction reference numbers.
  • Body Sizing & Fit Data: Size preferences and body measurements recorded for accurate sizing and virtual try-on features.
  • Device & Notification Data: Device identifiers and push notification tokens to send reservation updates and pickup reminders.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Process and manage rental reservations and schedule fitting appointments.
  • Provide tailored size suggestions and virtual wardrobe recommendations.
  • Send essential notifications regarding reservation status, pickups, and payment confirmations.
  • Enhance the security, integrity, and performance of our services.

4. Third-Party Payment Processing

All online payment transactions are handled through certified third-party payment gateways (including PayMongo). We never store raw credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time from the app settings. When an account deletion is initiated, your personal identification information is scrubbed and unlinked from past orders in accordance with accounting and statutory obligations.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical controls (including TLS encryption, row-level database security, and role-based access control) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your personal data, please contact our Privacy Team at privacy@jezsy.com or support@jezsy.com.
